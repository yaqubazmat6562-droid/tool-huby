Frequently Asked Questions

What is 4K resolution?

4K UHD resolution is 3840 × 2160 pixels — 4 times the pixel count of Full HD (1080p). It's the standard for modern TVs, monitors, and high-quality prints.

Will my image lose quality?

Upscaling always involves some interpolation. However, our advanced multi-step algorithm with optional sharpening produces the best possible result by minimizing blur and artifacts.

Is there a file size limit?

We recommend images under 25 MB for smooth processing. Very large images may take longer on mobile devices.

Do you store my images?

No! All processing happens 100% locally in your browser using HTML5 Canvas. Your images never leave your device and are never uploaded to any server.

Which format should I choose?

JPG — Best for photos (smaller file size). PNG — Best for graphics with transparency (larger). WebP — Modern format with great compression (best of both).
