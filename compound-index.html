How to Use Compound Interest Calculator

  • Initial Principal — starting investment amount enter karein.
  • Monthly Contribution (optional) — har mahine kitna add karna hai.
  • Interest Rate (annual) — expected return rate.
  • Time Period — years ya months mein select karein.
  • Compounding Frequency — Yearly, Half-Yearly, Quarterly, Monthly.
  • Live Calculation — koi bhi value change karte hi result update.
  • Maturity Amount, Total Interest, Growth % sab visible.
  • Donut Chart — Principal + Contributions vs Interest ka visual.
  • Yearly Breakdown — Har saal ka growth aur cumulative interest.
  • Frequency Comparison — Same investment with different compounding.
  • CSV Download se breakdown export karein.
  • Effective Annual Rate — compounding ka asar dekhein.
  • Sab kuch client-side hai — koi data server par nahi jata.
