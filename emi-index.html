How to Use Loan/EMI Calculator

  • Loan Type select karein: Home, Car, Personal, Education ya Business.
  • Loan Amount enter karein ya slider se adjust karein.
  • Interest Rate (annual) — bank jo rate de raha hai wo enter karein.
  • Loan Tenure — years ya months mein select karein.
  • Processing Fee (optional) — bank ke charges se effective cost dekhein.
  • Calculate EMI button dabayein — instant result milega.
  • Monthly EMI, Total Interest, Total Payment sab visible.
  • Donut Chart principal vs interest ka visual breakdown.
  • Amortization Schedule — har month ka breakdown (EMI, Principal, Interest, Balance).
  • CSV Download se poora schedule export karein.
  • Copy Result se summary clipboard mein.
  • Quick Presets se common loan scenarios ek click mein test karein.
  • Sab kuch client-side hai — koi data server par nahi jata.
